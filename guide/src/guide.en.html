01

Before you start

QR Lab turns a link, a contact or a Wi-Fi network into a QR code in the style of your slides, and a scientific article into a citation with a QR code to the paper.

In the browser

niki977.github.io/ultraspeaker-qr-lab, on computer and phone. QR codes download as PNG or SVG for PowerPoint, Keynote or a document; citations are copied.

In PowerPoint

Home tab → The Ultraspeaker group → QR Lab. The panel opens on the right and inserts QR codes and citations into the selected slide.

Coming soon

The PowerPoint panel is in testing and will be available shortly. This guide already describes it in full.

What stays on your device

Language, design, recent QR codes, templates and references stay in browser storage; in PowerPoint the references stay in the file. The Wi-Fi password is never saved.

03

Your first QR code

A QR code to your course page, for the last slide of your talk.

  1. Choose Link in step 1.
  2. Paste the address in step 2. The preview updates as you type.
  3. In Quick templates choose Ultraspeaker: black and white, the most readable.
  4. Set the Print width: 5 cm for a slide, 3 cm for an A4 poster.
  5. Click Download PNG. In PowerPoint the button is Insert into slide.

Other types of QR code

Eleven types: each one builds the content in the format phones recognise.

TextShows a short text.
EmailPrepares a message with recipient, subject and text.
Phone and SMSCalls or prepares a text message.
WhatsAppOpens a chat.
Wi-FiJoins the network with name, security and password.

SocialOpens a profile: the user name is enough.

Wi-Fi

The password is only used to create the code: it is never saved, not even in recent QR codes.

05

The design

The design changes the look, not the content: always test the scan with a phone.

  1. Style: Quick templates applies a complete style; My templates saves the design in two personal templates, plus the last two used.
  2. Style: choose the shape of the modules and of the eyes (outer frame and inner dot).
  3. Colours: module colour, gradient, eye colour, background or transparent. A warning appears if contrast is low.
  4. Logo: upload your logo (PNG, JPG, SVG) or choose an icon. Error correction switches to Max automatically.
  5. Frame: a frame with a call to action, such as “SCAN ME”.
  6. Advanced: error correction, margin and reset to the default design.
06

Scientific citations

In the Reference section a DOI, a PMID or a few words of the title become a citation with a QR code that opens the article.

  1. Choose Reference at the top.
  2. Type a DOI, a PMID, a PubMed or doi.org link, or title and author, and click Search.
  3. Check the preview: authors, title, journal, year. Warnings flag retracted or corrected articles, the open access version and duplicates.
  4. In Bibliography style choose Vancouver, APA 7, AMA or Harvard.
  5. In Citation on the slide choose the short, numeric or author-year format, or Full: the whole citation, as in the bibliography, without a number.
  1. 1Search. DOI, PMID, link or free text.
  2. 2Preview. Data come from Crossref, PubMed and Unpaywall.

Sources

Crossref, PubMed (NCBI) and Unpaywall, queried directly from your device. Warnings are an aid: always check important citations at the source.

07

The bibliography

Every article you insert or add goes into References in this presentation: numbering, the final slides and the PDF for the audience all start from there.

  1. Add without inserting. Add to bibliography puts the article in the list without changing the slides.
  2. Reuse. Click a reference: it returns to the preview, ready for another slide.
  3. Reorder. Drag a reference to change the order of the list and the PDF. On a phone, hold the dots on the left. Slide numbering does not change.
  4. Import a list. In “Import a list of DOIs or PMIDs” paste one DOI or PMID per line.
  5. Final slides. In PowerPoint Update numbering and citations renumbers by order on the slides; Create the “References” slides creates the bibliography slides. On the web Copy the bibliography copies it to the clipboard.

The bibliography PDF

Download PDF asks for the Lecture title (required), speaker and date, and what to put in each entry: Text, Link and QR turn on one by one, at least one stays on. «Clear the bibliography» empties the list in two clicks; the slides stay as they are. The PDF has white pages, an unnumbered list in the order you chose and “powered by The Ultraspeaker” at the bottom. On a phone you share it; from the PowerPoint panel it opens in the browser.

08

On the slide or in a file

The same QR code goes on the slide, in Keynote or in print: only the format changes.

Web version

Download PNG creates a 300 dpi image for the Print width you set; Download SVG (vector) stays sharp at any size. On a phone Save or share opens the share menu: Photos, WhatsApp, Mail. In Reference the main button copies the citation.

PowerPoint

Choose PNG or Vector and the Width on slide, then Insert into slide. For citations choose a layout, what to insert (Citation + QR, Citation only, QR only), position, font and colour: text and QR code are grouped at the bottom of the slide.

09

Speaker tips

A QR code only works if the audience can scan it from their seats.

Speaker tip

Leave the QR code on screen for at least 10 seconds and invite people out loud: “Scan the code to download the slides”. Without an invitation, almost nobody picks up their phone.

RoomDistance to the back rowQR side on the screen
Small roomup to 10 mat least 1/6 of the slide height
Conference room10–25 mat least 1/4 of the height
Plenaryover 25 mat least 1/3, alone on the slide

Citations

Put the citation at the bottom left, 14–16 pt, in grey: readable without stealing attention. The bibliography PDF, sent after the talk, is worth more than ten slides of references.

10

Frequently asked questions

Does the QR code expire?
No. It is static: it contains the content directly and works forever, without tracking.
The phone does not read the QR code.
Increase contrast, reduce the logo, choose a solid inner dot or enlarge the QR code.
The reference is not found.
Check the DOI or PMID. Newly published articles may not be on PubMed yet: try the DOI.
Where are the references kept?
In PowerPoint in the .pptx file; on the web in the browser you use.
I can't see QR Lab in the Home tab.
Follow the “Installation” page of the site, then reopen PowerPoint.
Requirements
Web version: a recent browser. Automatic placement, numbering and bibliography slides: an up-to-date PowerPoint for Microsoft 365. Search needs an internet connection.
