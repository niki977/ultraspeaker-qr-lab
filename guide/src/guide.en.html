01

Before you start

Image Lab adjusts the photos on your slides like a photo editor: levels, light, colour, crop, rotation and DuoTone, with the preview always in view.

In the browser

Open niki977.github.io/ultraspeaker-image-lab on a computer or phone. Load a photo, adjust it and download it: as a JPG, or as a PNG if it has transparent parts.

In PowerPoint

Home tab → The Ultraspeaker group → Image Lab. Select a photo on the slide and the pane replaces it with the adjusted version, keeping the original.

Coming soon

The PowerPoint pane is being tested and will be available soon. Chapter 08 already describes it in full.

Privacy

Photos are processed on your device and are never sent to a server. The browser only stores the language you choose, shared by all The Ultraspeaker Labs.

The interface

The preview stays at the top while you scroll through the adjustments; the buttons to compare and download stay at the bottom.

  1. 1Language and guide. Five languages; «Guide» opens this PDF.
  2. 2Preview. Shows every adjustment straight away. Drag a photo here to load it.
  3. 3Tools. Undo, Redo, Crop, Rotate and Duo & Tri Tone.
  4. 4Zoom. Drag the slider up; on a phone, pinch with two fingers.
  5. 5Try your own photo. Opens a photo from your computer or phone.
  6. 6Histogram. How the tones are spread, from black on the left to white on the right.
  7. 7Actions. Reset, Before / After and Download photo.
03

Adjust a photo

Start from a quick style, then fine-tune with the sliders while watching the preview.

  1. Choose a quick style. Stage raises contrast, shadows and sharpness for the projector; Enhance sets the levels for you; Black & white and Warm change the character of the photo.
  2. Light & colour. Exposure brightens or darkens everything, Contrast separates lights and shadows, Saturation doses the colours.
  3. Tones & detail. Highlights recovers a blown-out sky, Shadows opens up dark areas, Sharpness and Reduce noise work on the detail.
  4. White balance. Temperature goes from cool to warm, Tint from green to magenta.
  5. Double-click a slider to set it back to zero. Undo and Redo also work with ⌘Z / Ctrl+Z and ⇧⌘Z / Ctrl+Shift+Z.

Good to know

Quick styles only change light and colour: crop, rotation, «Enhance quality» and DuoTone stay as you set them.

04

Histogram, levels and neutral point

The histogram shows how many points of the photo sit in each tone. The three handles below decide where black starts, where mid-grey sits and where white ends.

  1. Read the histogram. If it is all on the left the photo is dark, if it is all on the right it is light. «SHADOWS CLIPPED» and «HIGHLIGHTS CLIPPED» warn you when detail is lost.
  2. Click Auto levels. Black and white move to where the data really starts: the photo gains contrast without blowing anything out.
  3. Fine-tune by hand. Drag BLACK to the right for fuller tones, MIDTONES to lighten or darken the greys, WHITE to the left for more light.
  4. Check a channel. RGB, R, G, B and LUM show all colours, a single colour or the luminance.
  1. Click Neutral point when the photo has a colour cast, for example the yellow light of a conference room.
  2. Tap the preview on a point that should be grey or white: a wall, a lab coat, a sheet of paper. Temperature and Tint adjust themselves.
05

Enhance quality and DuoTone

«Enhance quality» cleans up photos taken in low light; Duo & Tri Tone recolours the photo with the colours of your presentation.

Enhance quality

Click Enhance quality: Image Lab estimates the noise in the photo, reduces it while respecting the edges, sharpens the details and raises the local contrast. With Strength you choose how far to go; the photo size does not change.

  1. Open Duo & Tri Tone with the two-circle button: DuoTone switches on.
  2. Choose DuoTone or TriTone and one of the eight palettes.
  3. Customise the colours of Shadows, Midtones (TriTone only) and Highlights with the picker, the hex code or the eyedropper, which takes the colour from the original photo.
  4. Transparent on Shadows or Highlights makes that part transparent: the photo is saved as a PNG, useful for a logo or a silhouette on a coloured background.
  5. Done closes the panel; Remove removes the DuoTone.

Good to know

With DuoTone, saturation is not needed and disappears; Temperature and Tint become «Warm/cool filter» and «Green/magenta filter» and shift the light between the colours, like the filters used in black and white photography.

06

Crop and rotation

Each format only sets the aspect ratio: the photo keeps every pixel of the area you choose.

  1. Click Crop and choose the Format: Free, Original, an aspect ratio, a social media format, a slide or a print.
  2. Move the frame by dragging it and resize it from the corners. You can zoom into the preview: the frame stays still and the photo moves underneath.
  3. Rotate orientation switches between landscape and portrait.
  4. Apply crop confirms; Remove crop goes back to the whole photo.

  1. Click Rotate. The buttons turn the photo 90° left, 90° right or 180°.
  2. Straighten a crooked horizon by up to ±45° with the slider, the number field or the arrows: 0.5° per click, 0.1° with Shift.
  3. A grid appears and the photo zooms in just enough to leave no empty corners. Done closes the panel.
07

Compare and download

Before downloading, compare the result with the photo you started from.

  1. Before / After shows the original photo, labelled «ORIGINAL»; another click goes back to the result.
  2. Reset removes all adjustments, crop included.
  3. Download photo saves the adjusted version: a high-quality JPG, or a PNG if there are transparent parts.
  4. On a phone the button becomes Save or share: choose «Save Image» to add it to Photos, or send it with WhatsApp, Messages or Mail.
08

In PowerPoint

Coming soon

The PowerPoint pane is being tested and will be available soon. In the meantime, use the web version and insert the downloaded photo on your slide.

The pane has the same adjustments as the web version; only the last step changes: the photo goes back onto the slide.

  1. Select a photo on the slide and open Home → Image Lab. The line under the logo (1) says «Image selected».
  2. Adjust the photo while watching the preview (2): PowerPoint does not let add-ins change pixels live, so the slide stays as it is until step 3.
  3. Click Apply to slide (3). The adjusted photo replaces the original, with the same position, size and rotation; after a crop it stays centred in the same space.
  4. To change your mind select the photo again: the pane starts from the original with the saved adjustments. Original, with confirmation, puts back the photo you started from.
09

Speaker tips

A projector crushes the shadows and dulls the colours: a photo that looks perfect on your screen can turn grey in the room.

Speaker tip

For clinical photos start from Stage and check the histogram: important details should not sit in the first or last tenth. If the room is bright, set Shadows between +25% and +40%.

Speaker tip

Use the same adjustments for every photo in a sequence, for example before and after a treatment: same style, same temperature, same crop. The audience should notice the clinical result, not a change in the light.

10

Frequently asked questions

Does cropping reduce the resolution?
No. The social media, slide and print formats only set the aspect ratio. Only «Custom size…» resizes the photo to an exact size: if that size is larger than the area you chose, a warning tells you the photo will lose sharpness.
Why is the downloaded photo a PNG?
Because you switched on «Transparent» in DuoTone. When you share from a phone, the transparency may turn black or white.
The slide does not change while I move the sliders.
That is normal: PowerPoint updates the slide only with «Apply to slide». The preview in the pane shows the result straight away.
Where does the original photo go in PowerPoint?
It stays on the same slide, hidden: you can see it in Home → Arrange → Selection Pane. Keep it if you want to change the adjustments later.
The pane says «Update PowerPoint».
You need an up-to-date Microsoft 365: Windows 2601 or later, Mac 16.105 or later, or PowerPoint for the web. PowerPoint for iPad is not supported.
Are my photos uploaded to the internet?
No. Processing happens on your device, in the browser or in the pane.
How do I change the language?
From the menu at the top right. Your choice also applies to the other The Ultraspeaker Labs.
